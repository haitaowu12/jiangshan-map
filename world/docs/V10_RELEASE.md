# V10 release: connected lives and community histories

Version **0.9.0**. Prepared **2026-10-23**.

[Canonical GitHub branch](https://github.com/haitaowu12/jiangshan-map/tree/world-history-atlas/world) · [Live atlas](https://world-history-atlas-tony.techiesea.chatgpt.site) · [Final verification and publication status](V10_VERIFICATION.md) · [Current content manifest](../dist/data/content-expansion-v10.json)

## Scope

V10 continues the regional research outlined in the [V9 handoff](GPT_PRO_RESEARCH_HANDOFF.md). It adds **96 original bilingual entries**, including **39 developed biographies and 57 connected historical entries**, with **159 dated and located life milestones**. The new records cite **168 distinct URLs**. The emphasis is on people in their social and institutional settings: households, education, labour, migration, language, cultural production, political participation and collective action.

The four regional content files extend the existing shared atlas. They add no polity records, mapped routes or territorial geometry. A biography of a ruler, a record about a political assembly, or a history of independence organizing does not automatically become a political timeline bar. Representative points and biography milestones do not establish boundaries or a verified itinerary.

This is a selected expansion toward greater regional depth. It does not establish parity with the China collection, exhaustive world coverage or independent specialist verification of the entire atlas. The regional research registers document the inspected evidence, disputed claims, exclusions and remaining work.

## Evidence method and limits

Research was checked on **2026-10-23**. Each new source object carries its title, publisher, URL, claim-specific `evidenceNote`, `checkedAt` date and `retrieval` disclosure. Each record includes a bilingual `reviewLimit` and an explanation of what its dates and representative point mean. The `inspected-source-synthesis` label describes the editorial method; it does not turn an institutional narrative into universal agreement or certify all of the institution's underlying evidence.

The registers distinguish several levels of access:

- **Relevant article or document text inspected.** The stated passages support bounded claims. An inspected overview is not a claim to have read every archival document or manuscript it references.
- **Catalogue, collection description or abstract inspected.** These can establish an object's description, provenance, publication context or stated research result within their limits. They are not a full reading of the underlying manuscript, book or inaccessible article.
- **Limited indexed evidence.** The Philippine Commission on Women outcome statement is explicitly marked as search-indexed access after the page body returned 403. The enabling suffrage act was inspected directly. The two kinds of evidence are not described as equivalent.
- **Unavailable or conflicting material held for follow-up.** Failed pages are recorded as access gaps. Suspect chronology, unsupported superlatives and incompatible dates were omitted, narrowed or left as attributed disagreements.

Several entries use a single accessible institutional synthesis or related institutional sources. Two URLs from one museum, a university project or a national heritage service do not automatically supply independent corroboration. The release retains that limitation. Examples include Te Ara and NZHistory's shared institutional background, the linked Ban Chiang research programme, and multiple catalogue pages from one collection. A commemorative page can support a bounded date while providing an incomplete account of conflict, exclusion or policy outcomes.

Source disagreement remains visible where it affects interpretation. The Ifugao entry distinguishes an older heritage chronology from the research team's evidence for later intensification; the Plain of Jars entry separates jar emplacement from later mortuary use. Exact days are omitted when the inspected sources disagree. The Winnemucca record distinguishes her 1881 plea concerning Shoshone and Bannock prisoners from the later Paiute return petition. Institutional origin stories and claims of being the first in the world are not adopted merely because they appear on an authoritative website.

English and Chinese preserve the same scope and uncertainty. These are original editorial narratives, not reproductions of source paragraphs. This review does not claim original-language philological verification where the research used an English scholarly translation or institutional summary. Further community and specialist review remains valuable, especially for living traditions, contested histories and documentary interpretation. The governing requirements remain in [DATA_POLICY.md](DATA_POLICY.md) and [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Discovery and reading changes

**Curated connections appear on People pages.** The directory now combines explicit related-reading links, including reverse links, with existing textual mentions and journey associations. A biography can therefore lead to a connected community history even when that history does not repeat the person's name. These links remain reading associations, not proof of identity, personal presence or direct influence.

**Search accepts added unaccented aliases.** Displayed names preserve their diacritics, while aliases such as Apirana Ngata, Jose Marti and Emilie du Chatelet help readers find the same canonical person. Aliases do not create duplicate biographies or authorize new identity merges. English and Chinese names remain searchable in both map records and the People directory.

**Evidence scope appears beside sources.** New detail panels render the bilingual review-limit text with the existing source evidence. This lets readers see the boundary of a synthesis while reading it. The richer research registers retain the fuller claim and access ledger.

**The current manifest supplies one combined collection.** `content-expansion-v10.json` lists frozen V9 files and the four V10 files. The loader partitions biographies and other histories into the existing shared views, and the generated reading paths keep their prior IDs and entries. The application API and combined export identify version **0.9.0**.

**Changed asset URLs avoid reusing earlier module versions.** The page loads `app.mjs?v=10`; the changed content-expansion and discovery imports also use `?v=10`. This covers the application entry point and the changed child modules used to select the current manifest and display related reading. The app remains a static application with local data and no service worker.

## Verification and publication

The content build checks frozen V9 hashes, combined-ID uniqueness, related links, required evidence disclosures and biography sequence constraints before generating the current manifest and paths. The full validator also checks record structure, bilingual fields, coordinates, source URLs, course references, routes, licenses and geography. V10 regression tests cover preserved file metadata, inherited reading paths, bilingual and alias discovery, curated People links, share-state retention, biography sequencing and political timeline separation.

The final local build and check after the Chiri milestone correction passed **154 of 154 tests**. The validator reported **1,809 entries, 144 biographies, 418 milestones and 1,600 distinct cited URLs** in its whole-corpus inventory. Browser checks confirmed 245 People identities, an unaccented Nguyễn Thị Bình search resolving to one person with its curated context links, working reading-path navigation, and English/Chinese Chiri details with three life milestones ending in 1922 at a narrow 390 × 844 viewport. The checked revision, screenshots and publication outcome belong in [V10_VERIFICATION.md](V10_VERIFICATION.md). This release note does not assert an exact final commit or completed deployment. The canonical source and hosted application links remain at the top of this document.

Automated checks establish structural consistency and application behavior within their coverage. They do not establish historical truth, complete source independence, specialist agreement or the accuracy of every representative coordinate and historical polygon.

## Remaining priorities

The next research pass should deepen connected local sequences and broaden whose evidence appears in them:

- **Africa and West/Central Asia:** named lives and households in the Great Lakes, Congo basin, Sahel, Madagascar and additional Horn communities; local voices in Freetown land disputes; direct Geniza editions; critical work on Antoura and Navoi; the labour behind manuscript and textile production.
- **Asia:** archival corroboration for Dewi Sartika and Vaikom; worker and student testimony; independent assessment of education policies; competing Ban Chiang dating models; Malolos proceedings; community review of Ainu and Ifugao interpretation.
- **The Americas:** northern Indigenous community histories, Caribbean languages and women's organizing, and Brazil and the Andes beyond the selected writers and textile project. Juana Azurduy remains deferred pending stronger Bolivian scholarly and archival evidence for disputed biographical details.
- **Oceania:** Pacific local scholarship and New Guinea biographies or community histories; Banaban governance on Rabi, compensation litigation and continuing land claims; broader community sources for language and cultural transmission.
- **Europe:** eastern and southeastern Europe, Baltic and Scandinavian social histories, medieval urban and rural institutions, migration, labour and colonial connections; more primary texts and original-language scholarship beyond institutional syntheses.

These are substantive research gaps, not empty slots to fill with generic names or monuments. Further additions should continue to connect lives, communities, institutions and events while keeping the evidence for dates, places and interpretation inspectable.
