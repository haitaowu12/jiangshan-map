# V9: three editorial releases implemented

Prepared 23 October 2026. Application version **0.8.0**; the Site version number is a separate publication identifier.

The V8 baseline contained 1,369 selectable records and 24 full biographies. This release adds **344 original bilingual records**, including **81 full biographies**, **70 political overviews** and other social, cultural, event and place histories. The shared corpus now contains **1,713 records**, **105 full biographies** and **259 biography milestones**. The People directory combines three confirmed profile/biography overlaps and contains **206 identities**; a profile index is not a full biography.

All 32 roadmap workstreams have selected published material and an associated reading path. This means all three planned editorial releases are represented; it does not mean every proposed topic has been exhausted. The four pilot batches have 10 AF1, 13 EA1, 13 LA2 and 16 SE1 records. AF1 prioritizes ten connected political/material-context accounts rather than forcing the planning range or adding unrelated biographies to meet a quota. Biographies, source review and China corrections elsewhere in release 1 bring its total to 60.

## Evidence and independent research

The added records cite **649 distinct URLs**. Some pages were inspected in full; others supplied only indexed extracts after full-page failures. Each citation retains its actual access limitation. Several sources belong to the same institution or are derivative; two URLs are not automatically independent corroboration. No blanket expert historical or translation certification is claimed. English and Chinese narratives, date notes, facts and biography milestones were authored and checked together; quoted object titles and evidence metadata retain source language.

Regional research registers: [Africa](V9_AFRICA_RESEARCH.md), [East/Southeast Asia](V9_EAST_SOUTHEAST_RESEARCH.md), [South/West/Central Asia](V9_SOUTH_WEST_ASIA_RESEARCH.md), [Americas](V9_AMERICAS_RESEARCH.md), [Oceania](V9_OCEANIA_RESEARCH.md), [Europe](V9_EUROPE_RESEARCH.md) and [China](V9_CHINA_RESEARCH.md). The [cross-region critical review](V9_CROSS_REGION_REVIEW.md) records fifteen Europe/Oceania samples and focused Africa corrections.

The integrating editor independently inspected sources for Song agriculture and printing; Ban Zhao, Shen Kuo, Guo Shoujing and Xu Guangqi; UNESCO landscape/institution dossiers; Cai Lun's 105 presentation; Wang Jing's 69–70 works; uncertain Zheng He death chronology; the Galle inscription and Melaka depot. Later checks corrected Yaa Asantewaa's exile/death milestone to Seychelles, Tahirih's execution to Tehran, Tutu/Gbowee award context to Oslo and Yosano Akiko's publication/educational context to Tokyo. Waseda's original-edition catalog replaced an irrelevant Fukuzawa citation in Yosano's sources. Publication locations do not imply personal presence.

Gemini Pro sign-in, the selected Argon High mode and Deep Research were verified in the cloud browser. A global dossier brief and a focused chronology request were submitted. Both remained at “Generating research plan” without delivering a report during preparation. **No Gemini report was available or incorporated.** This is an external research-service limitation; the authored content relies on the separately inspected/indexed evidence above. The machine-readable research-service status records this explicitly and includes no private conversation URL or account identifier.

## China corrections and geography

The separate review layer now has six targeted event reviews: the two existing Dujiangyan/Bi Sheng reviews and four additional Cai Lun, Wang Jing, last-voyage and Zheng He death/location reviews. It retains all 24 chronicle annotations. Bi Sheng uses the Qingli 1041–1048 interval rather than a falsely precise 1047. Cai Lun's 105 court presentation is separated from much earlier archaeological paper. Wang Jing's 69–70 works are separated from a source river-course display window beginning in 11 CE. The final voyage's 1433 return is not proof of an immediate blanket maritime ban, and 1433/1435 death uncertainty does not establish a verified Calicut death site.

The held raw `china-landmark-412` conflates a Sri Lankan inscription and Melaka depot with a Champa-coast pin. It remains held in immutable source provenance. Two new, separately sourced records resolve the subject for readers: `galle-trilingual-inscription` and `melaka-ming-voyage-depot`. They preserve uncertain installation/foundation chronology rather than assigning an exact date. The two institutions disagree on the voyage associated with the Galle installation; neither is silently promoted to certainty.

The original pinned water data is now restored as **187 river paths, 62 lake edges and 41 bilingual labels**. Sixteen existing source corridors now draw as dated schematic lines at local zoom. Water windows use the source's start-inclusive/end-exclusive convention; corridor intervals are inclusive. Undated rivers are geographic references. Lake edges and river paths use line geometry to avoid accidental globe-complement fills. These are source-feature parity improvements, not independently reconstructed ancient hydrography or surveyed routes.

The political timeline now respects explicit `major`/`secondary` priority, including important secondary records that previously leaked into the key view. Only polities enter its bars; omitted bars remain explicitly expandable. The V8 winding repair and Taiwan/mainland shared display shade are retained.

## What remains open

- Detailed annual boundaries outside the reviewed source windows, especially premodern Africa, the Americas, South Asia and Oceania; new political prose does not authorize new polygons.
- Claim-by-claim corroboration of the 975 inherited events, the profiles and the river/corridor display windows; the six reviews and 24 annotations are bounded samples.
- Deeper local sequences within every broad region, including underrepresented communities, periods and lived experiences described in each research register. The 32 reading paths are editorial entry points, not complete regional curricula.
- Additional independently documented journey legs and local place identities. The existing 15 mapped route collections retain 126 stops; reading links are not new voyages.
- Source-access follow-up, more evidence from different institutions/communities, and external specialist review of contested chronology and bilingual terminology.
- A returned Gemini Deep Research report, if the service completes later, must be checked against primary evidence before adoption.
