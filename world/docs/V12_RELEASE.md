## Geography readers can use

An optional, source-dated Natural Earth layer adds clickable outlines for **50 US states, DC and all 13 Canadian provinces/territories**. Search a modern area, open its linked history, choose all dates or the selected year, and focus the map. Bilingual labels and keyboard activation are supported. The layer is a **2022-source modern reference**, separate from historical borders, Indigenous territories and treaty regions.

An explicit review of generalized coastlines led to 22 source-backed named-place associations. These preserve original coastal/island points while making relevant entries discoverable in their modern areas. Each association retains a method note and geographic evidence. There are currently linked entries in 39 of the 64 areas; this is selected coverage, with many gaps. See [geographic method and limits](V12_US_CANADA_GEOGRAPHY.md).
