# Next steps after the local V12 candidate

V12 adds US/Canada regional history and a modern geographic browser to the committed V11 baseline. Read [V12 release](V12_RELEASE.md), [verification](V12_VERIFICATION.md), the four research ledgers and [geographic method](V12_US_CANADA_GEOGRAPHY.md) before extending the collection. The shared atlas has 1,863 entries, 170 narrative biographies and 534 life milestones. These selected additions do not establish regional parity; China remains substantially denser.

1. **Continue the US/Canada regional sequences.** The modern browser now exposes collection gaps: 39 of 64 areas have linked entries, often only a few. Prioritize Atlantic Canada beyond Halifax/Grand-Pré (New Brunswick and Prince Edward Island), Yukon/Northwest Territories, the Great Lakes and Pacific Northwest, and the US Southeast, interior and island histories. Use bounded regional questions about work, water, migration, governance, learning and daily life; a familiar national figure is not a substitute for local history. Extend earlier Indigenous histories and living continuities with community/tribal sources and scholarship. Do not turn state/province cards into claims that current boundaries organize all earlier histories.
2. **Review the new lives and sources with regional, community and bilingual readers.** Champlain's uncertain birth, Sacagawea's differing accounts, Ware's ranch chronology, Carver's focus dates, institutional narratives and translation choices retain explicit limits. Seek Indigenous-language/community accounts, local archives and ordinary participants' experiences. Kinngait's cooperative, Saskatchewan clinics, Penn School, Hull-House and Acadian/Black Nova Scotian histories can be deepened without inventing complete life stories or assuming institutional aims prove outcomes. Source text inspection is not specialist acceptance.
3. **Treat geographic history as a separate research task.** The Natural Earth layer is modern orientation from a 2022 release. Research dated, reusable treaty, settlement, transport and political geometry for specific historical questions; preserve license, source-year and uncertainty. No narrative lifespan authorizes a border extension. Generalized coastlines omit valid points: use explicit reviewed named-place associations, never move points merely to pass polygon containment. Mount Hope remains unlinked pending a usable authoritative source; its Rhode Island preservation page was blocked by security verification. Do not bypass that block or count the search snippet as evidence.
4. **Deepen ancient and medieval local histories outside China.** V11/V12 lean toward early modern and modern institutions. Continue source-supported sequences in West/Central Africa, the Horn, West/Central Asia, South/Southeast Asia, the Americas and Oceania, with regional temporal frameworks and histories of households, food, work and environment. Keep the earlier V10/V11 continuity queue visible instead of organizing development as a famous-name quota.
5. **Carry forward unresolved earlier evidence.** Review Nwapa's publishing chronology, Behbudiy's birth/theatre chronology, Aisyiyah's preschool dates and Ukrainka's exact dates/title conventions. The Samoa source-only addendum exposes selected 1919 report OCR; collate it with facsimiles and Samoan accounts before treating colonial judgments or mortality estimates as settled. Philippine suffrage access remains limited; the Bessie Head catalogue follow-up corroborates editions without explaining every source discrepancy. Catalogue descriptions do not equal full manuscript inspection.
6. **Trial the connected reading activities.** Three regional paths gain fifteen source-comparison questions in V12. All 32 path IDs, prior stops/questions and 55 total readings remain intact. Ask learners to distinguish personal actions, collective work, source interpretation and missing evidence. Test whether area discovery and all-date/selected-year choices help them find and understand local histories. Application tests cannot establish historical learning or broad accessibility acceptance.
7. **Continue inherited-China claim review.** Six event reviews and 24 chronicle annotations remain targeted work. Keep the frozen source and held Galle/Melaka record inspectable; corrections belong in separate review layers. Independently establish the meaning of water/corridor display windows before describing them as construction or formation dates.
8. **Maintain the local source deliberately.** Work in `world/` on `mac/us-canada-atlas-depth`. Edit `expansion-*-v12.json`, run `npm run build:content`, then `npm run check`. The generator freezes V9–V11 narrative inputs and retains prior reading-path stops/questions. Edit geographic named-place evidence in `us-canada-area-links-v12.json`; the content generator rebuilds its history index. Rebuild geometry only from the exact pinned source with `build-us-canada-reference.mjs`. `npm run serve` starts the loopback preview. No GitHub push or hosted publication is part of this local task; reuse the existing Site identity if publication is later requested.

The V9 Gemini research requests did not return a report during preparation. Any later retrieved report is a research lead requiring source inspection. No external deep-research report is claimed for V11 or V12.
