# Next steps after V10

V10 extends the V9 collection with connected histories and life stories across the regional workstreams. See [V10 delivery](V10_RELEASE.md), [verification](V10_VERIFICATION.md) and the four V10 regional research registers for the exact additions and evidence decisions. Earlier release documents remain historical records. The China collection is still substantially denser; a broader distribution of new entries does not establish equal depth.

1. **Finish evidence depth before adding another bulk import.** Revisit indexed-only citations and same-institution source pairs, resolve specific disagreements, and invite regional and bilingual specialist review. Retain uncertainty when sources do not settle a date or interpretation. Do not call all inherited claims verified.
2. **Review geographic gaps separately.** Seek dated, reusable geometry for bounded historical questions outside China. Compare source maps, administrative meaning and dependencies; keep known wrong shapes excluded. Existing narrative lifespans cannot authorize carrying geometry across centuries. Preserve spherical winding checks and the requested shared Taiwan/mainland display shade.
3. **Extend local sequences within the 32 workstreams.** Continue from the V10 source registers rather than starting another disconnected list. Priority areas include West/Central African and Sahelian social history; West/Central Asian intellectual and urban networks; eastern and southeastern Europe; South Asian regional institutions and caste/labour histories; Japan, Korea and Southeast Asian local-language sources; Indigenous North American histories; Caribbean and Latin American everyday life; and Pacific community histories. Combine major turning points with work, food, environment and institutions. A course's presence is not proof of exhaustive coverage.
4. **Corroborate inherited China data.** Continue claim-level review beyond six event reviews and 24 chronicle annotations. The Galle/Melaka research conflation has two sourced replacement subjects; its original raw row remains held for audit. Independently test the restored water and corridor source windows before treating them as engineering or formation dates.
5. **Improve supported connections and learning activities.** Add journey legs only when a connection is documented. Test the 32 expanded regional reading paths with learners and deepen source-reading/comparison tasks; keep the main timeline focused on selected political periods. Biography milestones need a supported date and a clear place meaning. Approximate coordinates do not make an otherwise certain year approximate.
6. **Retrieve Gemini findings if the service returns them.** The signed-in Argon High Deep Research requests did not return a report during V9 preparation. A subsequent result is a research lead, not historical evidence by itself.
7. **Maintain the release source.** Keep the GitHub branch `world-history-atlas`, under `world/`, and the standalone Site source synchronized. The current application and validator read the same `CONTENT_MANIFEST` from `dist/content-expansion.mjs`. Run `npm run build:content` after regional V10 edits and then `npm run check`. Preserve V9 content files, imported source identities, attribution, licenses and geometry hashes. The original project outside `world/` remains separate.
